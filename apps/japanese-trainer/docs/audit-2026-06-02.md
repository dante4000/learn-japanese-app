## BUGS (to fix)

### B1 — Tap/click-to-reveal is broken in Kana **and** Kanji modes  (HIGH)
Clicking or tapping the character never reveals the answer. Verified in isolation
(both mouse click and touch `tap()`, both modes) — the `show` class is never left on.

**Impact:** On mobile (no hover) the answer cannot be revealed at all. The on-screen
copy even says "Tap or hover the character to reveal."

**Root cause:** the display element wires `focus→showAnswer`, `blur→hideAnswer`,
`mouseenter→show`, `mouseleave→hide`, and a `click` toggle that also refocuses the
input. On click: focus shows it → the click toggle sees it shown and hides it →
refocusing the input blurs the display and hides it again. Net result: hidden.
- Kana: `app.js` ~L796–813
- Kanji: `kanji.js` ~L422–437

**Fix:** decouple tap reveal from the focus/blur peek. Remove `focus`/`blur`
listeners (they also leak the answer on tab-focus); make `click` *reveal* (not
toggle) and keep hover (`mouseenter`/`mouseleave`) + keyboard (`keydown` Enter/Space).
